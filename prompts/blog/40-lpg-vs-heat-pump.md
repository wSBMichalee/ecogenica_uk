If you heat with LPG, you know the bill arrives in deliveries and swings with the market. Comparing **LPG vs heat pump running costs** fairly means looking at the cost of a unit of *heat*, not a litre of fuel. Here's the method, a worked example, and the table you can use with the price on your last LPG invoice.

## The method: cost per kWh of heat

**LPG:**
1. Price per litre (from your invoice).
2. Divide by the energy in a litre of LPG: about **7.08 kWh**.
3. Divide by your boiler's efficiency: **90%** for a modern condensing boiler, lower for older ones.

**Heat pump:**
1. Electricity price per kWh.
2. Divide by the heat pump's seasonal efficiency (SCOP). A well-designed system typically achieves around **3 to 4**.

## LPG vs heat pump at different LPG prices

| LPG price | LPG heat cost (90% boiler) | Heat pump on price cap, SCOP 3.5 |
|---|---|---|
| 50p / litre | 7.9p per kWh | 7.5p per kWh |
| 60p / litre | 9.4p per kWh | 7.5p per kWh |
| 70p / litre | 11.0p per kWh | 7.5p per kWh |
| 80p / litre | 12.6p per kWh | 7.5p per kWh |

*Electricity at 26.32p per kWh (Ofgem price cap, October to December 2026, Direct Debit). Heat pump tariffs with cheap windows can lower the effective rate further. [[DO POTWIERDZENIA: aktualna typowa cena LPG luzem dla domów (p/l) z wiarygodnego źródła]]*

## A worked example over a year

The same home, needing **8,550 kWh of heat a year**: what a 90% boiler gets from Ofgem's typical gas use of 9,500 kWh.

| Heating | Fuel used | Yearly fuel cost |
|---|---|---|
| LPG boiler at 60p / litre | about 1,342 litres | about £805 |
| LPG boiler at 70p / litre | about 1,342 litres | about £939 |
| Heat pump, SCOP 3.5 | about 2,443 kWh | about £643 |
| Heat pump, SCOP 3.0 | about 2,850 kWh | about £750 |

The heat pump's lower running cost is only part of the picture.

## Costs that don't show in the price per litre

- **Tank rental or ownership.** Many LPG customers pay rent for the tank, or will face maintenance and eventual replacement costs. [[DO POTWIERDZENIA: typowe koszty wynajmu zbiornika — źródło]]
- **Minimum deliveries and price swings.** You buy fuel when the tank runs low, not when prices are lowest.
- **Boiler servicing**, plus gas safety checks on the LPG installation.
- **A single bill.** With a heat pump, heating moves to your electricity bill. There's no second standing charge or delivery to manage.

## The grant: £9,000 for LPG homes

Until 31 March 2027, homes heated by LPG (or oil) with no mains gas connection can get **£9,000** off an air source heat pump through the Boiler Upgrade Scheme. After that it's due to fall back to £7,500. Details in [the £9,000 grant for oil and LPG homes](/guides/off-gas-grid-heat-pump-grant).

## When LPG still makes sense

If you've just installed a new LPG boiler, your home has very high heat loss that you can't reduce, or you pay an unusually low LPG price under a fixed contract, the case for switching right now may be weaker. Run the numbers with your own figures, and remember the £9,000 grant has an end date.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a heat pump cheaper to run than LPG?
At most LPG prices, yes. At about 50p per litre they're close on a standard electricity tariff; above that, a heat pump is cheaper per kWh of heat.

### What happens to my LPG tank?
If you rent it, the supplier collects it when your contract ends. If you own it, it can be removed. Check notice periods in your contract.

### Can I keep LPG for cooking?
Yes, though you may want to switch to electric cooking to end the LPG contract entirely.
