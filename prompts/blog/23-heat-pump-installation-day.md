A heat pump installation is bigger than a boiler swap, but it's well planned and predictable. Here's **what happens during a heat pump installation**, from the first visit to the moment you get the keys to your new system.

> **[callout: info]** Timings below are typical. Your installer will give you a schedule for your home. [[DO POTWIERDZENIA: typowy czas instalacji Ecogenica w dniach]]

## Before installation day

### Survey and design
A surveyor visits to measure each room, check radiators, pipework, the electrical supply and where the outdoor unit and cylinder will go. From this, the installer works out your home's heat loss room by room and designs the system.

### Quote and grant
You receive a quote with the Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant deducted. Once you accept, your MCS-certified installer applies to Ofgem for the grant voucher. Ofgem may contact you to confirm. The heat pump must then be commissioned within 120 days.

### Notifications
The installer notifies your electricity network operator and handles Building Regulations notification through their competent person scheme. In most cases in England, no planning application is needed. See [planning permission rules](/guides/heat-pump-planning-permission-rules).

### Preparing your home
- **Clear access** to the outdoor unit position, the cylinder location and radiators being replaced.
- **Move furniture** away from walls where pipework or radiators will change.
- **Plan for hot water downtime**: a gym shower, a friend's house, or a kettle for a day or two.
- **Pets and parking**: tell the team about both.

## Day one: outside and the cylinder

- **Old boiler out** (or isolated if it stays until changeover).
- **Base or brackets fitted** for the outdoor unit.
- **Outdoor unit positioned** on anti-vibration feet.
- **Pipework run** from the unit into the house, insulated and frost-protected.
- **Cylinder fitted** in its new position, with the old cylinder removed if you had one.

Expect heating and hot water to be off for at least part of the day.

## Day two (and three): radiators, pipework and electrics

- **Radiators swapped** in the rooms identified in the design.
- **Pipework changes** where needed, with lifted floorboards put back.
- **Electrical work**: dedicated circuit and isolator, controls and sensors.

## Final day: fill, test, commission

- **System filled, flushed and pressure tested.**
- **Commissioning**: the installer sets flow temperatures, the weather compensation curve, hot water schedule and legionella cycle, and balances the radiators so each room heats evenly.
- **Clean-up and making good** as agreed in your quote.

## Handover

You should come away with:

- **MCS certificate** for the installation.
- **Your settings explained**, ideally with the app set up on your phone.
- **Homeowner's manual** and a handover pack with system details.
- **Warranty registration**, which needs photos of the installation. With Ecogenica, the installer registers the product. Ask when it's done. [[DO POTWIERDZENIA: proces rejestracji gwarancji Ecogenica przez instalatora]]
- **Service schedule.** An annual service keeps the warranty valid.

## The first few weeks

Heat pumps feel different. Radiators are warm rather than hot, and the system runs for longer. Give it a week or two, then fine-tune with small changes. Our guide to [heat pump settings](/guides/heat-pump-settings-to-save-money) explains which ones matter.

## Frequently asked questions

### How disruptive is a heat pump installation?
Moderately. Most work is outside, at the cylinder and at radiators being replaced. Expect some lifted floorboards and short periods without heating and hot water.

### Will I have hot water during installation?
Usually not for part of the time, typically a day or so. Your installer will tell you when.

### Do I need to be home?
Someone should be there at the start and for handover. Agree access for the days in between.

### What if it's winter?
Installations happen year-round. Installers keep downtime short and can leave temporary heating if needed. [[DO POTWIERDZENIA: czy Ecogenica zapewnia grzejniki tymczasowe]]
