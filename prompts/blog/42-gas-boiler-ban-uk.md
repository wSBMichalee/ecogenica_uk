Search for **gas boiler ban** and you'll find headlines saying boilers are being banned in 2025, 2026 or 2035. None of them describe the law as it stands. In an existing UK home, you can still repair a gas boiler or replace it with a new one. What has changed is the rules for new homes, and the money on offer if you switch.

## Is there a gas boiler ban in the UK?

**Not for existing homes.** There is no law that stops you repairing or replacing a gas boiler, and nobody has to remove a working one. A replacement boiler in England must meet Building Regulations, including a minimum efficiency of 92% (ErP).

## What changes for new homes: the Future Homes Standard

The **Future Homes Standard** was published on 24 March 2026 and comes into force in England on **24 March 2027**.

- It sets carbon and energy standards for **new-build homes** that, in practice, mean low-carbon heating such as heat pumps or heat networks instead of gas boilers.
- **Transitional rules** apply to homes where plans were submitted before 24 March 2027 and work starts before 24 March 2028.
- It **does not apply to existing homes**, extensions to existing homes or boiler replacements.

Wales and Scotland set their own building standards.

## What happened to the 2035 date?

In 2021, the government's Heat and Buildings Strategy set an *ambition* to phase out new gas boiler installations from 2035. In September 2023, the government said households would only need to switch when replacing a boiler, and not before 2035. Neither statement became law, and the 2035 date doesn't appear in current policy.

## The Warm Homes Plan: incentives, not bans

The government's Warm Homes Plan, published in early 2026, sets **no phase-out date** for gas boilers in existing homes. It focuses on incentives:

- **£2.7 billion for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme to 2030**, keeping the £7,500 heat pump grant.
- A new **£2,500 grant for air-to-air heat pumps**.
- An aim of making heat pumps the natural choice when a heating system reaches the end of its life.

## What about oil and LPG boilers?

There's no legislated phase-out for oil or LPG boilers in existing homes either. But there is a stronger incentive to switch: until 31 March 2027, homes heated by oil or LPG with no mains gas connection can get **£9,000** off a heat pump. See [the £9,000 grant for oil and LPG homes](/guides/off-gas-grid-heat-pump-grant).

## So should you replace your gas boiler with a heat pump?

That's your choice, and it depends on your home and finances. The questions worth asking:

- **How old is your boiler?** If it's near the end of its life, the decision is coming anyway. Comparing both options before it fails gives you time.
- **What would it cost after the grant?** A heat pump typically costs more upfront than a boiler, and the £7,500 grant closes much of that gap.
- **What would it cost to run?** At October 2026 price cap rates, a well-designed heat pump costs about the same to run as a gas boiler per unit of heat, and you can drop the gas standing charge (about £108 a year) if you disconnect. See [heat pump running costs in winter](/guides/heat-pump-running-costs-winter).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are gas boilers being banned in 2026?
No. Nothing in 2026 law stops you installing a replacement gas boiler in an existing home.

### Are gas boilers banned in new builds?
Not by name, but from 24 March 2027 the Future Homes Standard effectively requires low-carbon heating in new homes in England, subject to transitional rules.

### Will I be forced to remove my gas boiler?
No. There's no requirement to remove a working gas boiler.

### Can I still get my gas boiler repaired?
Yes. Repairs and servicing are unaffected.
