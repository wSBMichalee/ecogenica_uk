A **heat pump quote breakdown** should let you see exactly what you're paying for. When quotes from different installers vary by thousands of pounds, the difference is usually in what's included, not in the heat pump itself. Here's every line a fair quote should cover, and the questions to ask before you sign.

## The lines a complete quote should include

### 1. The heat pump
Make, model and output, for example *Outback 8kW (ECO-ZR03FC), R290 monobloc*. The size should come from your home's heat loss, not a rule of thumb.

### 2. Hot water cylinder
Size in litres and type. A heat pump cylinder has a larger internal coil than a standard one. If you're replacing a combi, this is a new item; if you already have a cylinder, it will usually still need replacing.

### 3. Radiators
Which rooms, how many and what sizes. "Radiators included" should come with a list. See [do you need new radiators for a heat pump?](/guides/do-you-need-new-radiators-for-a-heat-pump)

### 4. Pipework and hydraulic parts
New or upgraded pipe runs, valves, filters, expansion vessel, and any buffer tank if the design needs one. Also frost protection for the pipes between the outdoor unit and the house.

### 5. Base and mounting
A concrete pad, ground frame or wall bracket, plus anti-vibration feet.

### 6. Electrical work
A dedicated circuit and isolator for the heat pump, any consumer unit work, and the notification to your electricity network operator. A three-phase upgrade (needed for some larger units) is a separate cost and usually involves the network operator.

### 7. Controls
Thermostat or app control, weather compensation sensor and any room sensors. Outback units include Wi-Fi app control.

### 8. Removal of the old system
Taking out the old boiler and, if you're coming off oil or LPG, decommissioning or removing the tank. Tank removal is often quoted separately, so check.

### 9. Commissioning and handover
Setting up the system, balancing radiators, showing you the controls, and a handover pack with manuals and settings.

### 10. Certification and paperwork
MCS certificate, Building Regulations notification, the Boiler Upgrade Scheme application and warranty registration.

### 11. Making good
Filling holes, patching plaster and tidying up after pipework changes. Some installers exclude redecoration; find out what "making good" covers.

## What the bottom of the quote should look like

| Line | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Total before grant | All of the items above |
| Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant | −£7,500, or −£9,000 for eligible oil/LPG homes until 31 March 2027 |
| VAT | 0% on the installation in Great Britain until 31 March 2027 |
| **You pay** | Total minus grant |
| Deposit and payment stages | How much, when, and what triggers each payment |

The grant is never paid to you. Your installer applies to Ofgem and deducts it from the price, and it should appear on both the quote and the invoice.

## The design figures that matter as much as the price

Two quotes at the same price can give very different running costs. Ask for:

- **Heat loss** of your home in kW, calculated room by room.
- **Design flow temperature**, for example 45°C. Lower means cheaper to run.
- **Expected seasonal efficiency (SCOP)** for your design.
- **Estimated annual running cost** and the assumptions behind it.
- **Noise assessment** showing the level at your neighbours' windows.

If an installer can't give you these, the system probably hasn't been designed for your home.

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. Is the price fixed, and what could change it?
2. Which radiators are being replaced, and why?
3. What's the warranty on the heat pump, the cylinder and the workmanship?
4. Who registers the warranty and when?
5. How long will I be without heating or hot water?
6. Are you MCS certified, and which consumer code do you belong to?
7. What servicing does the warranty require?

## Red flags in a heat pump quote

- One total figure with no breakdown.
- No heat loss calculation, or sizing "by the number of bedrooms".
- The grant shown as something you claim yourself after paying in full.
- Pressure to sign the same day.
- Vague "radiators as required" with no list.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do heat pump quotes vary so much?
Mostly because of what's included: radiators, cylinder, electrical work and tank removal. Compare line by line, not just the total.

### Is the grant deducted before or after VAT?
Installation is zero-rated for VAT until 31 March 2027, so in practice the grant comes off a VAT-free total.

### Should a quote include servicing?
Not usually, but ask what the first annual service costs, since servicing is often a warranty condition.
